# IO-VNBD data report

Generated by `python scripts/explore_iovnbd.py` from the raw CSV files. Every number below is computed by
that script; per-file and per-signal detail is in `results/data_report/`. Anything that does not pass an explicit check
is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

## 1. Files

- Location: `data/iovnbd/<drive>/S.csv` (smartphone) and `V.csv` (vehicle). The Stage 2 brief names `data/raw/iovnbd/`;
  that folder does not exist here. The data is at `data/iovnbd/` (`data.root` in the configs), downloaded by
  `make data` with SHA-256 checks against `configs/iovnbd_manifest.csv`.
- 72 drives, 144 files, 426 MB. Format: comma-separated text,
  one header row, no index column. Header units mix UTF-8 and Latin-1 bytes (e.g. `m/s²` appears as `m/s\ufffd`):
  72 of 144 files have such undecodable header characters.
- S.csv (phone): 24 columns; rows per file 156–126526 (total 1,070,745).
- V.csv (vehicle): 29 columns; rows per file 156–126527 (total 1,071,035).
- In 63 of 72 drives S.csv and V.csv have the same number of rows (they were exported as row-paired files).

## 2. Timestamps and sampling rate

| File | Timestamp column | Median Δt | Rate | Δt 1st–99th pct | Non-increasing steps | Gaps > 1 s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S.csv | `TIME SINCE START (ms)` (ms since logger start); also `DATE` wall clock | 100 ms | 10.00 Hz | 1–103 ms | 5468 | 3 |
| V.csv | `Time Since Start of Day (seconds)`; `Sample period (seconds)` | 100 ms | 10.00 Hz | 100–100 ms | 2 | 2 |

- Both files are sampled at about 10 Hz. `Sample period (seconds)` reads 0.10 s.
- Phone GNSS columns are repeated between fixes: latitude changes at a median 0.10 Hz (range 0.00–0.99); 69 drives update at ≤ 0.2 Hz, 3 at ≥ 0.5 Hz (Vta1a, Vta1b, Vta2).
- Clocks: phone wall clock (`DATE`) minus vehicle time-of-day is +0.7 s median (range -167.8 to +3913.8 s). It is a whole number of hours plus +0.5 s (median; range -167.8 to +313.8 s); 8 drives are +1 h and 64 are +0 h. A time-zone difference (e.g. local summer time vs UTC) would explain the whole hours. **UNVERIFIED.**
- S and V are exported row by row, but row pairing is **not** time-exact. Vehicle values are therefore looked up at the phone's time minus a clock offset, found per 600 s segment by correlating each phone gyro column with the car's yaw-rate sensor within ±15 s of that segment's row-pairing offset (the method of `src/data_io.py`). The correction to the row pairing is -1.6 s median per drive (range -9.7 to +13.9 s); 0 segments hit the search edge.
- 100 of 225 segments align with correlation ≥ 0.5 (both signals low-passed at 0.5 Hz); a segment between two aligned segments whose offsets agree within 0.5 s takes their mean offset. Together that covers 53% of phone samples. The rest are left unaligned (little turning to correlate, or no matching vehicle data); drives with < 20% aligned: Vta10, Vta11, Vta12, Vta13, Vta14, Vta15, Vta19, Vta1b, Vta20, Vta23, Vta24, Vta25, Vta26, Vta27, Vta3, Vta4, Vta5, Vta6, Vta7, Vta9, Vtb1, Vtb10, Vtb11, Vtb12, Vtb2, Vtb4, Vtb5, Vtb6, Vtb7, Vtb8, Vtb9, Vw1, Vw10, Vw12, Vw13, Vw14a, Vw14b, Vw15, Vw16a, Vw16b, Vw17, Vw5, Vw7, Vw8, Vw9, Y1. All vehicle-vs-phone checks below use aligned samples only.
- Phone `TIME SINCE START (ms)` has 5468 repeated or backwards steps in 7 drives (most: Vtb1 5456, Vta29 4, Y1 3, S4 2, M 1). The vehicle clock has 2.
- Phone GNSS speed lags the vehicle GNSS speed by 4.6 s median (range 0.2 to 5.4 s over 27 drives, best-lag search ±15 s after clock alignment; correlation at that lag 0.96 median).

## 4. Axis conventions (evidence)

**At rest.** Samples where the phone IMU is still (1 s std of |a| < 0.05 m/s², mean |ω| < 0.02 rad/s) *and* the car's speed sensor reads < 0.1 m/s: 5,521 samples in 17 drives with ≥ 50 such samples.

| | acc X | acc Y | acc Z | norm | phone pitch (ORIENTATION) | phone roll |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Median over drives | 0.09 | -0.08 | 9.85 | 9.86 | -86° | -59° |
| Range over drives | -0.32…0.40 | -0.69…0.22 | 9.83…9.87 | 9.84…9.88 | -88…-81° | -141…63° |

- **acc Z reads +g at rest** (within 0.3 m/s² of 9.81 in 100% of drives); X and Y read about 0. Sign: Z is **positive up** (reads +g, i.e. specific force) — VERIFIED.
- But the phone's own ORIENTATION reports a pitch beyond ±30° in 100% of these drives, i.e. the phone was not lying flat. A body-frame accelerometer would then split g between axes. It does not, so the logged X/Y/Z are **not body-frame**: they are already rotated into a level frame by the logger.
- The `GRAVITY X/Y/Z` columns are constant (max per-file std 0.076 m/s², Z mean 9.8065), consistent with the same levelling. They carry no information.

**Straight-line acceleration and braking.** Samples with |car yaw rate| < 2°/s, car speed > 2 m/s and |d(speed)/dt| > 0.5 m/s² (92,978 samples, 26 drives). Reference: the time derivative of the car's indicated speed.

Driver E's accelerometer tracks the car's forward acceleration much more weakly after de-rotation (median 0.31) than drivers A and B (0.70 / 0.78). A noisier phone or a less reliable phone azimuth would both explain it; the cause is **UNVERIFIED**.

- No single raw axis tracks forward acceleration across drives (median |r| X 0.24, Y 0.15, Z 0.03): the forward direction moves between X and Y with the car's heading, as expected for an Earth-referenced horizontal frame.
- After de-rotation by the phone azimuth, one axis tracks forward acceleration (best-axis median |r| 0.55). It is X in 4 drives and Y in 22 drives, with slope -0.62 (median), so the forward axis and its sign depend on how the phone was mounted in each drive. The mapping "raw X/Y = East/North" itself is **UNVERIFIED**: which Earth axis X points to depends on the logger's convention, and the sign/permutation in the East/North table is not consistent enough to state it.
- Gyroscope: the column that best matches the car's yaw-rate sensor is `GYROSCOPE Pitch (rad/s)` in 26 of 26 drives (median r +0.70); median |r| for the other columns: `GYROSCOPE Yaw` 0.06, `GYROSCOPE Roll` 0.12. Slope against the car's yaw rate (rad/s): median +1.01, so this column is in rad/s and has the same sign convention as the car's yaw-rate sensor. The header calls it Pitch; the label is wrong for the yaw axis. Unlike the accelerometer, the gyro is **not** levelled (it is a body-frame sensor); the assignment of the two horizontal gyro axes is UNVERIFIED.

- **Wheel speed** (header: rad/sec) equals the indicated speed in km/h (ratio 0.9987, range 0.9973–0.9998 over drives). If it really were rad/s, the implied rolling radius would be 0.278 m for every drive — plausible for a small car, but an exact 1/3.6 coincidence. It is more likely km/h with a wrong header. **UNVERIFIED** either way.
- **V `Height (km)`** has a median of 99; a height of ~100 km is impossible, so the header unit is wrong. It is probably metres. The phone's GPS altitude is higher by a near-constant 50 m (median; range 47 to 61 m). That is close to the geoid–ellipsoid separation in the UK, which would fit `Height` = metres above mean sea level and the phone = metres above the WGS-84 ellipsoid. **UNVERIFIED.**
- **Phone `GPS SPEED (Kmh)` is m/s, not km/h**: against the car's GNSS speed in km/h its slope is 0.275 (1/3.6 = 0.278). The header unit is wrong.
- **Phone GNSS updates about every 10 s** (0.10 Hz): latitude, longitude, altitude, speed and course stay constant between updates and are repeated on every 10 Hz row. Holding a value for ~10 s alone delays it by ~5 s on average, which is the size of the phone-GNSS lag found in §2 (4.6 s); whether the lag is entirely the update interval is **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Heading** is degrees clockwise from north (median abs(heading − track course) 0.8°). The car's `Yaw Rate` has the opposite sign to d(Heading)/dt (slope -0.87): **yaw rate is positive counter-clockwise (left turn)**. Its magnitude matches d(Heading)/dt to 0.87× (the heading derivative is smoothed).
- **`GPS SATELLITES IN RANGE`** is text like `27 / 28`. The first number is ≤ the second in 100% of rows (median over drives), consistent with "used / in view". **UNVERIFIED.**
- Not checked (header unit taken as given, **UNVERIFIED**): steering angle, gear, engine speed, temperatures, brake pressure and positions, pedal position, battery voltage, `GPS ACCURACY (m)`, `Vertical velocity`.

## 7. UNVERIFIED items

- Earth-frame convention of raw accelerometer X/Y (which of X/Y points East/North, and their signs).
- Forward/lateral body axis after de-rotation: differs per drive (phone mount), so it must be estimated per drive.
- Why driver E's accelerometer tracks forward acceleration only weakly after de-rotation.
- The two horizontal gyroscope axes (`GYROSCOPE Yaw`, `GYROSCOPE Roll` headers): only the vertical axis is identified.
- Wheel speed unit (header rad/sec; data matches km/h).
- Cause of the phone-GNSS lag (consistent with its ~10 s update interval, not proven).
- Cause of the phone/vehicle clock offset (close to a whole hour, i.e. possibly a time-zone difference).
- Vehicle `Height (km)` unit (cannot be km; metres likely; datum unknown) and phone `GPS ALTITUDE` datum.
- `GPS SATELLITES IN RANGE` meaning of the two numbers.
- Scenario of each drive.
- All CAN signals not listed in §5 (steering, gear, engine speed, pedals, brake, temperatures, battery).

## 8. Consistency with the existing pipeline

`src/data_io.py` (used for the reported MVP) already: de-rotates accelerometer X/Y by `ORIENTATION (Yaw)`; uses `GYROSCOPE Pitch` as the vertical (yaw) gyro axis and only the magnitude of the other two; never uses wheel speed or CAN signals as inputs; re-synchronises phone and vehicle clocks per 600 s segment by gyro/yaw-rate cross-correlation; and, in phone-GNSS mode, uses `GPS SPEED (Kmh)` without a km/h conversion, i.e. as m/s. The findings above agree with each of these choices.
